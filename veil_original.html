How to Play

You are the Veil. Bias mutations with Influence, then advance cycles. You never fully control the form.

Win by raising permanence (top-left bar) until the world cannot erase you cheaply — or by overwhelming it.

Lose if the countermeasure bar fills while you are still weak, or presence collapses.

Transmission spreads. Resilience fights seals. Concealment slows detection. Surge is power with a price.
