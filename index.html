What the numbers mean

Influence is your currency. You start with 7 and earn about +2.5 every cycle at first, rising as you spread (more presence, more regions, and high detection all help). Spend it on mutations and adaptations; prices are shown on each button. Unspent Influence carries over.

Presence is how much of the world carries you.

Detection is how much the world knows. High detection speeds up the cure and border closures.

Counter is the world's cure progress. At 100% you are erased unless you are already permanent.

Stability is how steady your form is. Low stability means random drift in your traits.

Permanence is your progress toward becoming impossible to remove.

How to Play

You are the Veil. Bias mutations with Influence (your currency: you earn it every cycle and spend it on mutations), then advance cycles. You never fully control the form.

Win by raising permanence (top-left bar) until the world cannot erase you cheaply — or by overwhelming it.

Lose if the countermeasure bar fills before you are permanent, or every reservoir is purged.

Transmission spreads. Resilience fights seals. Concealment slows detection. Surge is power with a price. Sealed, detected regions get purged: Resilience softens it, Concealment keeps you off the radar.
